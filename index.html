Tenstorrent From Zero

A novice-to-expert series on Tenstorrent AI accelerators. It covers why they exist, how the hardware works, how to program it, and how to verify every idea on the ttsim simulator.

Every concept chapter has interactive animations, real code from tt-metal and ttsim, a self-check quiz, and further reading. Every lab was run on the simulator, and the output it shows is the real output.

Pinned sources: tt-metal 7aad8aac, ttsim b3620244 (v1.15.0), tt-isa-documentation be833101. The content was checked against these commits in October 2026.
